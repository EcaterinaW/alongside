ABOUT ALONGSIDE

A place to find support when you need it most.

Alongside is an informational platform created for parents and guardians facing the difficult experience of childhood cancer. Our goal is to make reliable support resources easier to find and help families feel less alone.

WHY ALONGSIDE

Because finding support shouldn't feel like another challenge.

A childhood cancer diagnosis can bring many challenges at once. Families may have to navigate unfamiliar information, unexpected financial difficulties, emotional stress, and difficult decisions.

Finding trustworthy resources can be overwhelming, especially during an already difficult time.

Alongside was created to bring important support information together in one place, so families can spend less time searching and more time focusing on what matters most.

HOW TO USE
THIS WEBSITE

Start with what your family needs right now.

Alongside is organized into four main areas of support. You can explore the section that is most relevant to your situation and then choose a region to find organizations and resources.

Each regional page provides information about organizations that may offer support. Visit the official website of each organization to learn more about its services, eligibility requirements, and current contact information.

i

An informational resource

Alongside does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, legal advice, or professional psychological care. The information on this website is intended to help families discover available support and resources. Eligibility requirements and available services can change, so please check each organization's official website for the most current information.
