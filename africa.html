FINANCIAL SUPPORT · AFRICA

Support for families across Africa.

Finding financial and practical support can be difficult while a child is receiving cancer treatment. These organizations support children with cancer and their families through assistance such as accommodation, transportation, food, medical expenses and other essential needs.

AFRICA

Organizations that may help.

The resources below are official organizations that provide support for children with cancer and their families in different African countries. Eligibility and available services vary by organization.

A note about financial assistance

Every organization has its own eligibility requirements, application process and available services. Some forms of support may require a referral from a hospital, social worker or healthcare team. Funding and programs can also change over time, so please check the organization's official website for the most current information before applying.

ALONGSIDE

You don't have to navigate this alone.

Financial difficulties can add another layer of stress during a child's treatment. We hope these resources make it a little easier to find practical support and focus on what matters most: being there for your child.
