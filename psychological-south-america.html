PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPPORT · SOUTH AMERICA

Emotional support for children and families.

Childhood cancer can affect the emotional wellbeing of the whole family. These organisations offer psychological, emotional and psychosocial support for children, parents, siblings and caregivers.

FIND SUPPORT

Organisations that can help.

The resources below provide psychological, emotional or psychosocial support specifically connected to childhood cancer and its impact on children and families.

01

Fundación Natalí Dafne Flexer

Provides psychological support for children and young people with cancer, as well as guidance and emotional support for parents, grandparents and other family members. The foundation also runs parent groups, bereavement groups and support for young cancer survivors.

Fundación Natalí Dafne Flexer →
Argentina
02

Fundación María Cecilia

Provides psychological and emotional support to children with cancer and their families throughout treatment, including personalised care from psycho-oncology professionals and emotional support for parents and caregivers.

Fundación María Cecilia →
Argentina
03

Fundação Sara

Offers psychological follow-up and emotional support to children and adolescents undergoing cancer treatment. Its multidisciplinary programmes also include recreational, educational and social support for families.

Fundação Sara →
Brazil
04

Fundación Nuestros Hijos

Provides psychological care for children with cancer and their families as part of its medical and social support programmes, including emotional support during palliative care and throughout the treatment journey.

Fundación Nuestros Hijos →
Chile
05

Fundación Camino

Provides individual and group psychological support for adolescents with cancer and their caregivers, guided by a psycho-oncology professional. Its programmes also include recreational activities and spaces for conversation and emotional connection.

Fundación Camino →
Chile
06

Oncomamás

Runs a dedicated mental health programme for parents and caregivers of children with cancer, offering emotional accompaniment, psychosocial support, group spaces and specialised psycho-oncology guidance.

Oncomamás →

Supports children and adolescents with cancer and their families through psychological, social and emotional support, helping families navigate treatment and its broader impact.

A note about psychological support

The type and availability of psychological support varies between organisations. Some programmes provide direct counselling or psycho-oncology services, while others offer group support, emotional accompaniment or family-centred programmes. Check each organisation's official website for current eligibility, location and contact information.
