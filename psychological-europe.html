PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPPORT · EUROPE

Emotional support for children and families.

Childhood cancer can affect the emotional wellbeing of the whole family. These organisations provide psychological, emotional and psychosocial support for children, parents, siblings and caregivers.

FIND SUPPORT

Organisations that can help.

The resources below were selected because their official websites describe psychological, psychosocial, emotional or family support connected to childhood cancer.

01

Österreichische Kinderkrebshilfe

Supports children and families affected by childhood cancer through psychological and psychosocial services, including funding for psychotherapeutic care and aftercare programmes for children, adolescents, parents and siblings.

02

PSAPOH — Psychosocial Care in Paediatric Oncology

A professional network supporting psychosocial care for children and adolescents with cancer and their families. Its resources cover psychological and psychosocial support throughout treatment, rehabilitation, and follow-up care, with psychosocial teams working in pediatric oncology centres.

PSAPOH / GPOH →

Provides professional psychological support to children and young people with cancer, parents, siblings and survivors. Sessions can be individual or family-based and available in person or remotely.

A note about psychological support

The type of support varies between organisations. Some provide direct psychological or psychotherapeutic care, while others offer psychosocial counselling, peer support, family programmes or connections to professional services. Availability can also depend on the child's location, age and treatment centre. Check the organisation's official website for current eligibility and contact information.
