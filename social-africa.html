Africa

Organizations and programs that may help families affected by childhood cancer with accommodation, transportation, meals, education, family services, community support, and other everyday needs.

SOCIAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below support children and young people with cancer and their families in different parts of Africa. Services and eligibility vary by country and location, so always check the organization's official website for current information.

01

Rainbows & Smiles →

Supports children with cancer and their families with practical assistance such as transport to treatment, household support, hospital care items, family advocacy, and social activities that bring children and families together.

South Africa
02

Hope for Cancer Kids →

Helps families overcome practical barriers to childhood cancer treatment through transportation, accommodation, nutrition, family assistance, access to care, and support for children and caregivers throughout treatment.

Kenya
03

Bless a Child Foundation →

Provides free accommodation for children with cancer and caregivers, nutritious meals, transportation to hospitals, educational support, play activities, and other essential services while children receive treatment.

Uganda
04

Lifeline for Childhood Cancer Ghana →

Supports children with cancer and affected families by helping improve access to care and providing family support, food and essential resources, advocacy, and services intended to reduce the burdens families experience during treatment.

Ghana
05

TAPCCO →

Tesfa Addis Parents Childhood Cancer Organization provides families travelling for treatment with accommodation, nutritional support, transportation to the hospital, family activities, and practical assistance throughout a child's treatment.

Ethiopia
06

Tumaini La Maisha →

Provides non-medical support for children with cancer and their families, including a home-away-from-home family hostel, transportation, nutrition, child and parent education, play activities, and other support during treatment.

Tanzania
07

Okapi Children Cancer Foundation →

Works as a support system for children diagnosed with cancer and their families through family-centered programs, community activities, practical support, awareness, and initiatives that encourage inclusion and reduce isolation during treatment.

Nigeria
08

Association L'Avenir →

A parent and family organization supporting children with cancer and their families. Its Maison de l'Avenir provides accommodation for families who must travel to Rabat from other parts of Morocco for their child's treatment.

Morocco
09

Kayula Childhood Cancer Foundation →

Provides non-clinical support for children with cancer and life-threatening blood disorders and their caregivers, helping families manage the practical and social challenges associated with childhood cancer treatment.

Zambia
10

KidzCan Zimbabwe →

Provides free support to children with cancer and families, including accommodation through Rainbow Children's Village, transportation to treatment, nutritional support, food assistance, and family-centered services.

Zimbabwe
11

Cancer Association of Namibia – CHICA →

Through its CHICA program, the Cancer Association of Namibia supports children with cancer and their families, including free accommodation at the CHICA Interim Home for children who must travel to Windhoek for treatment.

Namibia
12

Health & Hope Oasis →

A supportive recreational center for children with cancer and their families, offering a safe environment away from the hospital where children, parents, and siblings can spend time together between periods of treatment.

Egypt
BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about social support

Social services, eligibility requirements, referral procedures, and program availability vary widely across Africa. Some organizations work with particular hospitals or treatment centers, and accommodation or transportation may depend on availability. Visit the organization's official website or speak with your child's healthcare or social-work team to confirm which services are currently available to your family.

AFRICA

You don't have to navigate this alone.

A child's healthcare team, hospital social worker, or local childhood cancer organization can also help connect your family with housing, transportation, food, education, family programs, and community support.
