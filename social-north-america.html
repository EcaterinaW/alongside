SOCIAL SUPPORT · NORTH AMERICA

North America

Childhood cancer affects much more than medical treatment. Families may need a place to stay, transportation, meals, help navigating services, support for siblings, and connection with people who understand what they are going through. These organizations provide practical and social support to children and families across North America.

Organizations that can help

Services and eligibility vary by location. Visit each organization's website to find the programs available in your community.

01
NORTH AMERICA

Ronald McDonald House Charities

Ronald McDonald House Charities helps families stay close to a child receiving medical care. Local programs may provide accommodation near hospitals, meals, hospital Family Rooms and other services that help families stay together during treatment.

  • Family accommodation near hospitals
  • Meals and everyday essentials
  • Ronald McDonald Family Rooms
  • Sibling and family resources
  • Community and peer connection
Visit RMHC →
02
UNITED STATES

CancerCare for Kids

CancerCare for Kids provides free professional support for children, teenagers and families affected by cancer. Oncology social workers can help families understand their options, locate resources and manage some of the practical and social challenges that come with cancer.

  • Oncology social workers
  • Resource navigation
  • Support groups and family programs
  • Guidance for parents and caregivers
  • Support related to school and everyday life
Visit CancerCare →
03
CANADA

Childhood Cancer Canada

Childhood Cancer Canada supports children with cancer and their families through programs designed for the realities of childhood cancer. Its work includes resources for newly diagnosed families, community programs and support during and after treatment.

  • Programs for children and families
  • Resources for newly diagnosed families
  • Community connection
  • Support during and after treatment
  • Resources for survivors and caregivers
Visit Childhood Cancer Canada →
04
CANADA

Canadian Cancer Society

The Canadian Cancer Society connects families with services throughout Canada. Support can include transportation to treatment, accommodation, community resources and programs designed for children, young people and families affected by cancer.

  • Transportation to cancer treatment
  • Accommodation during treatment
  • Child, youth and family programs
  • Recreation and camp programs
  • Community Services Locator
Visit Canadian Cancer Society →
05
MEXICO

AMANC

Asociación Mexicana de Ayuda a Niños con Cáncer supports children and adolescents with cancer and their families in Mexico. Its comprehensive support model helps families with many of the practical needs that arise while a child is receiving treatment.

  • Temporary accommodation
  • Food and meals
  • Transportation
  • Access to medicines and other resources
  • Psychosocial and family support
Visit AMANC →
06
MEXICO

Casa de la Amistad

Casa de la Amistad supports children and young people receiving cancer treatment in Mexico, including families who have traveled away from home to access care. Its programs address practical needs while also helping children continue learning and family life during treatment.

  • Family lodging
  • Meals and essential supplies
  • Transportation support
  • Educational support
  • Emotional and family support
Visit Casa de la Amistad →

Please note: Programs, locations, eligibility requirements and available services may change. Families should contact each organization directly to confirm current services in their area.

No family should have to navigate childhood cancer alone.

Support can also mean a meal, a ride, a place to stay, or simply having someone beside you.
