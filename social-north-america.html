SOCIAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below provide different forms of practical and social support for children with cancer and their families. Available services and eligibility vary by location, so always check the organization's official website for current information.

01

Ronald McDonald House Charities →

Helps families stay close to a child receiving medical care through accommodation near hospitals, meals, Family Rooms, and other services that support families during treatment.

North America
02

CancerCare for Kids →

Free professional support for children, teenagers, parents, and caregivers affected by cancer, including oncology social workers, support groups, information, and resource navigation.

United States
03

Childhood Cancer Canada →

Programs and resources for children with cancer and their families, including support for newly diagnosed families, community connection, and resources during and after treatment.

Canada
04

Canadian Cancer Society →

Connects families with transportation, accommodation, community services, information, and programs that can help children, young people, and families during cancer treatment.

Canada
05

AMANC →

Comprehensive support for children and adolescents with cancer and their families, including accommodation, meals, transportation, access to resources, and psychosocial support.

06

Casa de la Amistad →

Supports children and young people receiving cancer treatment and their families with practical services such as lodging, meals, transportation, education, and family support.

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about social support

Available services, locations, and eligibility requirements can change. Some programs may require a referral through a hospital social worker or healthcare team. Before contacting an organization, visit its official website and check which services are currently available for your family and location.

NORTH AMERICA

You don't have to navigate this alone.

A child's healthcare team or hospital social worker can also help connect your family with local housing, transportation, family programs, and community resources.
