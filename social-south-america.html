South America

Organizations and programs that may help families affected by childhood cancer with accommodation, transportation, family services, education, community support, and other everyday needs.

SOCIAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below support children and adolescents with cancer and their families in different parts of South America. Services and eligibility vary by location, so always check the organization's official website for current information.

01

Fundación Natalí Dafne Flexer →

Provides free comprehensive support for children and young people with cancer and their families, including treatment guidance, family assistance, educational activities, and support programs throughout the cancer journey.

Argentina
02

GRAACC →

Supports children and adolescents with cancer and their families through specialized care, social assistance, and accommodation for patients and families who need a place to stay during treatment.

Brazil
03

Fundación Nuestros Hijos →

Supports children and young people with cancer and their families through social programs, rehabilitation, education, psychological support, and family accommodation during treatment.

Chile
04

Fundación SANAR →

Provides comprehensive support for children and adolescents with cancer and their families through psychological assistance, social support, family guidance, and psychosocial accompaniment.

Colombia
05

Magia →

Helps children with cancer and their families, including families who travel to Lima for treatment, by providing accommodation, meals, transportation, emotional support, and other practical help.

Peru
06

Fundación Pérez Scremini →

Provides comprehensive care for children and adolescents with cancer and supports families who travel to Montevideo, including accommodation and psychosocial assistance during treatment.

Uruguay
07

Fundación Sol en los Andes →

Supports children with cancer and their families in Ecuador, providing practical assistance and a supportive environment intended to make the treatment journey easier for families.

Ecuador
08

ASPANICC →

A parent-led organization supporting children with cancer and their families through psychosocial assistance, family accompaniment, and help connecting families with resources needed during treatment.

Bolivia
09

FANAC →

Fundación de Apoyo a Niños y Adolescentes con Cáncer supports families in Paraguay and works to improve the quality of life of children and adolescents facing cancer.

Paraguay
10

Fundanica Zulia →

Provides support for children and adolescents with cancer and their families, including emotional and social support, education, food, transportation, accommodation, and help navigating care.

Venezuela
11

TUCCA →

Supports children and adolescents with cancer and their families. Social assistance may include meals for patients and caregivers, food support, and help for families adapting to treatment away from home.

Brazil
12

Fundación Camila Salomé →

Helps children with cancer and other serious illnesses continue their education when treatment prevents regular school attendance, while also providing social and emotional support to children and their families.

Ecuador
BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about social support

Available programs, locations, referral procedures, and eligibility requirements can change. Some organizations work directly with specific hospitals or require an evaluation or referral from a healthcare or social-work team. Visit the organization's official website to confirm the services currently available to your family.

SOUTH AMERICA

You don't have to navigate this alone.

A child's hospital or social-work team can also help connect your family with local housing, transportation, educational programs, family services, and community organizations.
