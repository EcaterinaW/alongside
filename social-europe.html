SOCIAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below support children and young people with cancer and their families in different parts of Europe. Services and eligibility vary by country and location, so always check the organization's official website for current information.

01

Young Lives vs Cancer →

Provides specialist social workers who help children, young people, and families navigate the practical and social impact of cancer, including accommodation, education, family needs, benefits, and connections to local services.

United Kingdom
02

Cancer Fund for Children →

Provides children, young people, siblings, and families affected by cancer with social and emotional support, community-based services, peer connection, family programs, and therapeutic short breaks.

Ireland
03

Fundación Aladina →

Supports children and adolescents with cancer and their families through emotional, psychological, and practical programs, hospital activities, family assistance, and opportunities for children to participate in camps.

Spain
04

Peter Pan ODV →

Welcomes children and adolescents receiving cancer treatment in Rome and their families, providing free family accommodation and a supportive community designed to make long periods away from home easier.

Italy
05

Provides additional support for children with cancer and their families through family activities, peer connection, sibling programs, hospital support, recreation, and other services focused on family wellbeing.

Belgium
06

A parents' association supporting children with cancer and their families throughout Greece. Services include family accommodation in Athens, home-care support, information, and assistance with the social challenges of treatment.

Greece
07

Supports children and adolescents with cancer and their families through counseling, parent networks, family programs, sibling support, camps, educational assistance, and connections to local organizations.

Germany

Portugal

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about social support

Social services, eligibility requirements, referral procedures, and program availability vary across Europe. Some organizations work with specific hospitals, regions, or national healthcare systems. Visit the organization's official website or speak with your child's healthcare or social-work team to confirm which services are available to your family.

EUROPE

You don't have to navigate this alone.

A child's healthcare team, hospital social worker, or local childhood cancer organization can also help connect your family with housing, education, family programs, peer support, and community resources.
