SOCIAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below support children and young people with cancer and their families in different parts of Europe. Services and eligibility vary by country and location, so always check the organization's official website for current information.

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about social support

Social services, eligibility requirements, referral procedures, and program availability vary across Europe. Some organizations work with specific hospitals, regions, or national healthcare systems. Visit the organization's official website or speak with your child's healthcare or social-work team to confirm which services are available to your family.

EUROPE

You don't have to navigate this alone.

A child's healthcare team, hospital social worker, or local childhood cancer organization can also help connect your family with housing, education, family programs, peer support, and community resources.
