LEGAL SUPPORT · SOUTH AMERICA

South America

Organizations and programs that may help families affected by childhood cancer understand their rights, access benefits, navigate healthcare systems, and find legal or advocacy support across South America.

LEGAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below provide different forms of legal information, advocacy, rights education, benefits guidance, and family support. Not every organization provides direct legal representation, so always check the organization's official website for current services and eligibility.

01

Argentina — Childhood Cancer Rights →

Argentina — Law 27.674 establishes a comprehensive protection system for children and adolescents with cancer. It includes rights related to healthcare, education, transportation, financial assistance, housing, and caregiver protections.

Argentina
02

Instituto Nacional del Cáncer →

Argentina — official information about the rights and benefits provided under Law 27.674, including healthcare coverage, financial assistance, transportation, housing, education, and caregiver leave.

Argentina
03

Fundación Natalí Dafne Flexer →

Argentina — nonprofit organization supporting children and young people with cancer and their families through information, guidance, family support, and assistance in navigating treatment and social resources.

Argentina
04

CONIACC →

Brazil — national confederation of organizations working with children and adolescents with cancer. It advocates for the rights and needs of children with cancer and their families and supports organizations across Brazil.

Brazil
05

Brazil — Statute of Rights of People with Cancer →

Brazil — Federal Law 14.238 establishes rights for people with cancer, including access to healthcare, social and legal assistance, priority treatment, and special protections for children and adolescents.

Brazil
06

Brazil — Ministry of Health →

Brazil — official information about access to cancer treatment through the public health system. The Ministry confirms that people with suspected or diagnosed cancer can receive oncology care through SUS, including specialized pediatric oncology services.

Brazil
07

Chile — Ley SANNA →

Chile — the SANNA system provides protected leave and a financial subsidy for eligible working parents and caregivers who need to care for a child affected by cancer or another serious health condition.

Chile
08

Corporación Oncomamás →

Chile — organization supporting parents of children with cancer through family guidance, information, advocacy, and support related to treatment access and caregiver protections.

Chile
09

Fundación Nuestros Hijos →

Chile — nonprofit organization supporting children with cancer and their families through medical, rehabilitation, social, educational, and family support programs.

Chile
10

Colombia — Ley Jacobo →

Colombia — legislation establishing priority, comprehensive, and continuous care for children and adolescents with cancer, with measures intended to support timely diagnosis and treatment.

Colombia
11

Fundación Colombiana de Leucemia y Linfoma →

Colombia — provides support and guidance to children, young people, parents, and caregivers affected by blood cancers, including help addressing barriers to healthcare, social support, education, and patient rights.

Colombia
12

Defensoría del Pueblo — Peru →

Peru — the national Ombudsman's Office monitors and promotes the rights of children and adolescents with cancer. Its official information addresses timely access to pediatric oncology treatment, implementation of childhood cancer laws, and protection of healthcare rights.

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about legal support

Legal rights, public benefits, caregiver leave, healthcare coverage, and available services vary between countries and may also depend on the family's circumstances. Some organizations listed here provide direct family support or advocacy rather than legal representation. Before contacting an organization, visit its official website and check the current requirements, eligibility rules, and services available in your location.
