Asia

Organizations and networks that may help families affected by childhood cancer understand patient rights, access appropriate healthcare, find advocacy support, and navigate family and healthcare systems across Asia.

LEGAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below work on childhood cancer advocacy, patient and family support, access to healthcare, policy, and rights-related issues. Some provide direct family services, while others focus on advocacy, information, referrals, or improving healthcare systems. Always check the organization's official website for current services and eligibility.

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about legal support

Legal rights, public healthcare systems, insurance rules, education protections, social benefits, and available family services vary significantly between Asian countries. Some organizations listed here provide direct family assistance, while others focus on advocacy, healthcare access, information, policy, or referrals. Before contacting an organization, visit its official website and check which services are currently available for your family and location.

ASIA

You don't have to navigate this alone.

Your child's healthcare team, hospital social worker, patient advocate, or local childhood cancer organization may also be able to help your family understand available rights, benefits, and local support resources.
