PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPPORT · ASIA

Support for the emotional side of childhood cancer.

These organizations provide psychological, emotional, psychosocial, or family support for children with cancer and the people caring for them. Some offer direct counselling and therapy, while others provide child-life services, support groups, or professional guidance.

FIND SUPPORT

Resources across Asia.

Support can look different from one organization to another. Some services are available directly to children and parents, while others work through hospitals, social workers, or specialized pediatric oncology programs.

01

CanKids KidsCan

India’s pediatric psycho-oncology program provides psychological counselling, teletherapy, expressive therapies, parent support groups, child-life interventions, psychoeducation, and bereavement support for children with cancer, caregivers, and survivors.

A note about psychological support

Services vary by organization, location, eligibility, and treatment stage. Some organizations provide direct counselling or therapy, while others offer psychosocial support, child-life programs, peer groups, or referrals to specialists. Please check the official website before contacting an organization, as programs and availability can change.

You don't have to navigate this alone.

Finding emotional support can be an important part of caring for a child with cancer — and for taking care of yourself, too.
