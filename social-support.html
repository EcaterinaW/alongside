SOCIAL SUPPORT

Practical support for families navigating everyday challenges.

Finding support during childhood cancer can involve much more than medical care. Families may need help with accommodation, transport, meals, childcare, social services, and everyday practical needs. Alongside brings together organizations that may help families navigate these challenges.

A note about social support

Social support services can vary significantly between countries and regions. Some organizations provide direct assistance to families, while others connect families with social workers, community services, accommodation, transportation, or other practical resources. Please check each organization's official website to learn about current services and eligibility requirements.
