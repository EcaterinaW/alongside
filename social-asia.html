Organizations and programs that may help families affected by childhood cancer with accommodation, transportation, education, family services, community programs, and other everyday needs.

SOCIAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below support children and young people with cancer and their families in different parts of Asia. Services and eligibility vary by location, so always check the organization's official website for current information.

Shine On! Kids →

India

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about social support

Social services, eligibility requirements, referral procedures, and program availability vary widely across Asia. Some organizations work with specific hospitals, cities, or healthcare systems. Visit the organization's official website or speak with your child's healthcare or social-work team to confirm which services are currently available to your family.

ASIA

You don't have to navigate this alone.

A child's healthcare team, hospital social worker, or local childhood cancer organization can also help connect your family with housing, transportation, education, family programs, and community resources.
