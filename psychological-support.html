A note about psychological support

Every family responds to a cancer diagnosis differently. Psychological and psychosocial support can include professional counselling, child-life services, peer support, family programmes, support for siblings and help from social workers. If a child or family member is experiencing persistent distress, anxiety or changes in mood, consider speaking with the child's healthcare team about appropriate professional support.
