PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPPORT · AFRICA

Support for the emotional side of childhood cancer.

These organizations provide psychological, emotional, psychosocial, or family support for children with cancer and the people caring for them. Some offer direct counselling and therapy, while others provide psychosocial programs, support groups, or family-centered care.

FIND SUPPORT

Resources across Africa.

Psychological support can take many forms during childhood cancer. Depending on the organization, families may find counselling, psychosocial care, therapeutic activities, peer support, or help coping with the emotional impact of diagnosis and treatment.

01

CHOC Childhood Cancer Foundation

CHOC provides professional psychosocial and emotional support through social workers, counsellors, and social auxiliary workers based in paediatric oncology units. Support is available to children and families throughout the cancer journey, including bereavement support.

KidzCan Zimbabwe

Provides holistic psychosocial care addressing the emotional and social burden of childhood cancer. The organization supports children and families alongside its medical, practical, accommodation, transport, and case-management services.

Hope for Cancer Kids

Provides psychosocial support to children with cancer and their caregivers in Kenya. Its program includes art therapy for children, caregiver support groups, coping mechanisms, and activities designed to reduce isolation and help families manage the emotional impact of treatment.

A note about psychological support

Services vary by organization, location, eligibility, and treatment stage. Some organizations provide direct counselling or psychosocial care, while others offer family support, therapeutic activities, support groups, or referral and navigation services. Please check the official website before contacting an organization, as programs and availability can change.

You don't have to navigate this alone.

Finding emotional support can be an important part of caring for a child with cancer — and for taking care of yourself, too.
