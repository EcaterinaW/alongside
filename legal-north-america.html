LEGAL SUPPORT · NORTH AMERICA

North America

Legal information, advocacy, and support resources for families navigating childhood cancer in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Some organizations provide direct legal assistance, while others help families understand rights, benefits, insurance, employment protections, or available support systems.

Before seeking legal support

Legal systems, eligibility rules, public benefits, insurance protections, and available services differ between countries, states, provinces, and territories. Not every organization listed here provides direct legal representation. Some provide legal information, advocacy, navigation, or referrals to appropriate services. Always check the organization's official website for current eligibility and contact information.
