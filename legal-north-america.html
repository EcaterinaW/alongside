LEGAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below provide different forms of legal information, advocacy, rights education, benefits guidance, and family support. Not every organization provides direct legal representation, so always check the organization's official website for current services and eligibility.

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about legal support

Legal rights, public benefits, insurance rules, education protections, and available services vary between countries, states, provinces, and territories. Some organizations listed here provide direct legal services, while others focus on advocacy, information, benefits navigation, or referrals. Before contacting an organization, visit its official website and check which services are currently available for your family and location.
