Organizations and networks that may help families affected by childhood cancer understand patient rights, access appropriate healthcare, navigate European systems, and find advocacy or legal support across Europe.

LEGAL RESOURCES

Support for families.

The organizations below work on patient rights, childhood cancer advocacy, access to healthcare, policy, family support, and rights-related information. Some may provide direct assistance or referrals, while others focus on advocacy and information. Always check the organization's official website for current services and eligibility.

SIOP Europe →

The European Society for Paediatric Oncology connects families, patient organizations, healthcare professionals, and policymakers. Its work includes advocacy for better policies and access to care for children and adolescents with cancer across Europe.

BEFORE REACHING OUT

A note about legal support

Legal rights, public benefits, healthcare rules, education protections, insurance systems, and available family services vary between European countries. Some organizations listed here provide direct assistance, while others focus on advocacy, patient rights, information, benefits navigation, or referrals. Before contacting an organization, visit its official website and check which services are currently available for your family and location.

EUROPE

You don't have to navigate this alone.

Your child's healthcare team, hospital social worker, patient advocate, or national childhood cancer organization may also be able to help your family understand available rights, benefits, and local legal or advocacy resources.
