PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPPORT · NORTH AMERICA

Emotional support for children and families.

Childhood cancer can affect the emotional wellbeing of the whole family. These organisations offer counselling, psychosocial care, support groups, family programmes and other resources to help children, parents, siblings and caregivers cope.

FIND SUPPORT

Organisations that can help.

The resources below were selected because their official websites specifically describe emotional, psychological, psychosocial or family support connected to childhood cancer.

01

St. Jude Children's Research Hospital

Provides psychosocial services and family support throughout a child's illness. The team includes psychologists, social workers, child-life specialists, music therapists and other professionals who support children, siblings, parents and caregivers.

A note about psychological support

Psychological and psychosocial services can vary by location, age, diagnosis and stage of treatment. Some organisations serve families only within specific states or through participating hospitals. Always check the organisation's official website and eligibility requirements before contacting or applying for support.
