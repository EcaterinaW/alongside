FINANCIAL SUPPORT · AUSTRALIA & OCEANIA

Support for families across Australia & Oceania.

These organisations provide financial, practical, accommodation, emotional, or family support for children and young people affected by cancer and the people caring for them.

FIND SUPPORT

Resources for families.

Explore organisations across Australia and New Zealand that may help families manage some of the practical and financial challenges that can come with childhood cancer.

01

Redkite

Provides financial assistance to families caring for a child with cancer, including help with rent, mortgage, groceries, petrol, electricity, phone bills, car costs and treatment-related accommodation.

Australia
02

Kids With Cancer Foundation Australia

Offers financial assistance for families of children aged 0–18 receiving treatment for cancer, helping with household bills, rent or mortgage, groceries, petrol, car expenses, phone and internet costs, and other essential expenses.

Kids With Cancer Foundation Australia →
Australia
03

Childhood Cancer Association

Supports children with cancer and their families in South Australia and the Northern Territory through financial assistance, accommodation, counselling, education and practical family support.

Camp Quality

Provides free camps, family experiences, counselling and other support programs for children and families affected by cancer, helping families reconnect and cope throughout the cancer journey.

Canteen Australia

A note about financial assistance

Eligibility, application procedures and available services can change. Some organisations may require confirmation from a hospital, healthcare professional or social worker. Always check the organisation’s official website for the most current information before applying.
